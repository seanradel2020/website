I'm Sean, an engineer who is just as happy on the water or in the kitchen as I am solving a technical problem. I enjoy learning new things, spending time outdoors, and bringing people together over a good meal.

## What I like to do

- Cook from scratch, especially pizza, pasta, and bread
- Explore wine pairings and local craft breweries
- Hike New England trails
- Fish, sail, and surf
- Play soccer and golf
- Stay involved in wrestling and volunteer coaching
- Garden and grow something I can bring to the table
