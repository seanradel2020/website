Experience & skills

I'm a Senior IT Systems Engineer at Palo Alto Networks, working as an SRE supporting multiple platforms across FedRAMP, AWS, and GCP environments. I support my organization as an expert in cloud governance, FinOps, and security. I deploy my systems across multiple cloud providers, using CI/CD tools like Harness and Jenkins. I'm a platform engineer for multiple systems for internal customers that support our business functions.

I began studying cybersecurity at the University of Maine in 2020 with the Univeristy of Maine Cybersecurity Team, training in network, Linux, and Windows security and competing in CTF and collegiate competitions. I served as team captain and club president, earned two third place NECCDC finishes, and contributed to three research labs focused on AI and privacy compliance, human-computer interaction, and server infrastructure.

I started my career with an internship at the US Government, then moving to General Dynamics Electric Boat, and then a final internship at CyberArk, where I went on to begin my career full-time.
